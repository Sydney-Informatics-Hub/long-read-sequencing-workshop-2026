# Sample datasets

## Where do these samples come from?

The samples used in this training come from a real genomic surveillance study: a retrospective analysis of carbapenemase-producing Gram-negative bacteria isolated at Cambridge University Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust (CUH) in the UK between 2014 and 2020 (n=165). The study's own framing lines up closely with this training's outbreak scenario — most isolates were hospital-onset or healthcare-associated infections, and most carbapenemase-producing organisms were carriage isolates picked up through routine screening, with 71% of isolates coming from rectal swabs — the same route by which the isolate in this training's own scenario was first detected.

## Sample preparation

Isolates were long-read sequenced using a MinION device and R9.4.1 flow cells. The majority were multiplexed (3–12 isolates per flow cell) using the SQK-RBK004 rapid barcoding kit. All FAST5 reads were basecalled and demultiplexed with Guppy v3.6.0, using the "high-accuracy" basecalling model and the relevant barcoding kit for demultiplexing.
