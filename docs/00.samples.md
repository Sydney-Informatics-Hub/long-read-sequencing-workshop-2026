# Sample datasets

This training draws on two batches of samples, referred to throughout by their accession prefixes: **ERR\*** and **SRR\***. Both were sequenced on Oxford Nanopore MinION devices using R9.4.1 flow cells, but differ in library prep and basecalling settings — details worth knowing before polishing with medaka, since the right `medaka_consensus` model depends on exactly this kind of information: the pore/flow cell chemistry, the sequencing device, and the basecaller version and mode used.

| Sample group | Device | Flow cell | Guppy version | Basecalling mode | Medaka version |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ERR\* samples | MinION | R9.4.1 | v3.6.0 | high-accuracy | version not stated |
| SRR\* samples | MinION (FLO-MIN106, SpotON), basecalled via a MinIT device | R9.4.1 | version not stated | fast | v0.8.1 |
