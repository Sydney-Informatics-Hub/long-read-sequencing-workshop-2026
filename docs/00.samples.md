# Sample datasets

## What we'll actually work with

This training follows one sample end to end, from raw reads through to annotation: the **ERR\*** samples from a real hospital genomic surveillance study (below), which map directly onto this training's own outbreak scenario. For the QC and preprocessing modules specifically, we'll also work hands-on with two additional, unrelated example datasets — **barcode01** and **PBK97200** — chosen for their contrasting quality profiles.

## The ERR\* samples: a hospital carbapenemase surveillance study

The ERR\* samples come from a retrospective analysis of carbapenemase-producing Gram-negative bacteria isolated at Cambridge University Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust (CUH) in the UK between 2014 and 2020 (n=165). 

## barcode01 and PBK97200: the QC/preprocessing examples

**barcode01** comes from a separate study demonstrating ONT-based finishing of bacterial genomes: 12 *Klebsiella pneumoniae* isolates multiplexed and sequenced together on a single MinION flow cell, then assembled using a combination of ONT long reads and pre-existing Illumina short reads.

**PBK97200** comes from a benchmarking dataset built to test a bacterial genome workflow's reproducibility: 24 isolates spanning 15 species (across genera including *Bacillus*, *Klebsiella*, *Salmonella*, and *Staphylococcus*), each run in quadruplicate on a single PromethION flow cell.

## How each dataset was generated

|  | ERR\* samples | barcode01 | PBK97200 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Device | MinION | MinION Mk1B | PromethION 2 Integrated (P2i) |
| Flow cell / chemistry | R9.4.1 | FLO-MIN106, R9.4 | FLO-PRO114M, R10.4.1 |
| Library / barcoding kit | SQK-RBK004 rapid barcoding | SQK-LSK108 ligation kit | SQK-RBK114.96 Rapid Barcoding Kit 96 V14 |
| Basecaller | Guppy v3.6.0, high-accuracy model | Albacore v1.1.2 | SUP@5.2.0 model (MinKNOW v25.11.0) |
